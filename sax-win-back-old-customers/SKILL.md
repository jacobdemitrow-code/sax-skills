# Win Back Old Customers — $150 + $30/mo

**FIRST:** follow the sax-core skill (rules, ask-first list, privacy, prices).
**BEFORE "DONE":** run the sax-done-check skill.

CLAUDE'S RECIPE:
- Collect: the client's past-customer list (name + phone), their usual services, and any special offer they're willing to give.
- Write 2 bring-back texts in the client's voice: (a) a friendly "it's been a while" text, (b) a seasonal or special-offer text. Each under 300 characters, with the business name, the offer, and a booking link or phone number.
- Build a private "Bring-Back" page on their Ullbek site (same style as the lead dashboard): each past customer listed with a one-tap Text button (prefilled message), a Mark Sent button, and a sent counter saved on the phone.
- Set a reminder for the client to do a bring-back round every 60–90 days.
- WARNING: texting has legal rules (consent). Only text people who already did business with them. Always include "Reply STOP to opt out."
- Test: open the page on a phone, tap one Text button, and confirm the message fills in correctly.

JACOB'S GUIDE:
1. Ask the client for their list of past customers (name + phone).
2. I write the bring-back texts in their voice and build their one-tap Bring-Back page.
3. Show them how to tap Text, then Mark Sent.
4. Remind them: only text real past customers.

## EXTRA SAFEGUARDS FOR THIS SERVICE
- PRIVACY: the past-customer list must be locked: the client's Google Sheet or a password-locked page. Never on an open page (sax-core section 3).
- The client sends the texts from their own phone (service agreement, section 5).
