# Lead Generation Page — their own page of local leads

**FIRST:** follow the sax-core skill (rules, ask-first list, privacy, prices).
**BEFORE "DONE":** run the sax-done-check skill.

PRICE: $300 to start + $40/month. Included in the Everything Bundle.

COLLECT: the client's trade, the cities they serve, and their best kind of customer.
CLAUDE'S RECIPE:
- Research: web-search for real local businesses or people who would buy from them (example: property managers for a cleaning business). Check each one is real and current, with a working phone number and a real website or listing. Check local rules that change who's worth targeting (example: Menifee bans Airbnb-style short-term rentals, so property managers beat Airbnb hosts there).
- Pick the 10 best: a mix of small/easy wins and bigger accounts. Tag each Small or Big.
- Build a private page on their Ullbek site (leads.html, the same as Come Home to Clean's). Each lead gets name, city, phone, a short note on why they're a fit, one-tap Call and Text buttons, an editable prefilled pitch, a Mark Sent button, and a contacted counter saved on the phone.
- Test: open it on a phone, tap Call and Text on one lead, and confirm Mark Sent saves after a refresh.
JACOB'S GUIDE:
1. Tell me the client's trade and area.
2. I find and check the leads, then build their lead page.
3. Show them how to use the Call/Text buttons and Mark Sent.

## EXTRA SAFEGUARDS FOR THIS SERVICE
- PRIVACY: put the leads page behind a password, or keep the list in the client's Google Sheet.
- Texting businesses that never contacted you has its own legal rules. Until Jacob's lawyer has looked at this, the page offers Call first and Text second.
