COLLECT: Google review link, website/booking link, Facebook (or Instagram) link, business phone, logo/colors.
CLAUDE'S RECIPE:

- Write a Facebook caption and save it as a Google Doc for easy copy-paste.
- Optional upsell: printable leave-behind card or counter sign.
- Scan-test the code on a real phone before handing it over.

1. Get the client's Google review link, website, Facebook, and phone number.

4. I make the Facebook post and caption; you show the client the preview.
5. The client posts it (or you post it with them). Offer printed cards as an extra.
