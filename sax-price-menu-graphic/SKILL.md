# Price Menu Graphic — $60 one time (add-on)

**FIRST:** follow the sax-core skill (rules, ask-first list, privacy, prices).
**BEFORE "DONE":** run the sax-done-check skill.

PRICE: $60 one time. Add-on. NOT in the Everything Bundle.

CLAUDE'S RECIPE:
- Collect: the client's price list (each service + its "starting at" price), logo/colors, phone number, and booking link.
- Design a square Facebook graphic in Canva: business name + logo at top, each service with its "starting at" price, phone and "Book now" at the bottom, in the client's colors. Keep it big and easy to read on a phone.
- Claude does every design step itself (upload logo, place text, set colors). Show Jacob a preview in the chat before it's final.
- Export it as a PNG with a plain public link, and write a short Facebook caption saved as a Google Doc for easy copy-paste.
- Honest limit: posting and pinning needs the client (or Jacob with the client) logged into their Facebook page. Claude makes the graphic and caption; a person posts and pins it.
- Check: open the PNG on a phone and confirm every price matches the client's price list (and their price calculator, if they have one).
- If their prices change, the graphic, the caption, and the price calculator all need the new numbers.

JACOB'S GUIDE:
1. Get the client's price list, logo, and phone number.
2. I design the price menu and show you a preview.
3. You check the prices with the client.
4. Post it on their Facebook page together, then tap the three dots on the post and choose "Pin to top."

## EXTRA SAFEGUARDS FOR THIS SERVICE
- ASK FIRST before posting or pinning on the client's Facebook.
