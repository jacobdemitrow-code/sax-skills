# Get More Reviews — $150 + $30/mo

**FIRST:** follow the sax-core skill (rules, ask-first list, privacy, prices).
**BEFORE "DONE":** run the sax-done-check skill.

CLAUDE'S RECIPE:
- Collect: the client's Google review link (Google Business Profile > Ask for reviews), and what they want to offer for referrals (if anything).
- Write 2 texts: (a) review request — thanks them, gives the direct Google review link; (b) referral text — "know someone who needs us?" plus the offer.
- Add a "Job Done" step to their lead tracker: when a job is marked Done, a Send Review Text button appears with the message already filled in.
- If they have the One-Scan QR code, the review button there points to the same link.
- Optional fully-automatic version (later): Make sends the review text when a job is marked Done. Needs a business phone service for texting. Test before selling.
- Test: tap the review link on a phone and confirm it opens the client's Google review box.

JACOB'S GUIDE:

2. I write the review + referral texts and add the Send Review Text button to their tracker.
3. Show them: after every job, mark it Done, then tap Send Review Text.
