# Never Miss a Lead — $400 + $50/mo

**FIRST:** follow the sax-core skill (rules, ask-first list, privacy, prices).
**BEFORE "DONE":** run the sax-done-check skill.

CLAUDE'S RECIPE:
- Facebook auto-reply: Business Suite > Inbox > Automations > Instant reply ON (Messenger + Instagram). Paste a custom instant-reply message written for THIS client's business. Check if Business AI is available and turn it on.
- Booking form: build on the client's Ullbek site. Use the form_submit skill. Fields: name, phone, callback number, address, job, details, preferred date, promo code. Submissions auto-email the owner. Send one test booking to confirm it arrives.
- Lead tracker (CRM): a private phone page — name, number, what they want, price, status (new/quoted/booked/done/paid), who to call back today.
- Honest limit: the Facebook part needs the client (or Jacob with the client) logged in. Claude writes the message; a person pastes it.
- Optional add-on: missed-call text-back. Only works with a business phone service (like Quo), not a regular cell number.

JACOB'S GUIDE:
1. Get the client's Facebook page access (or sit with them while they log in).
2. Turn on the instant reply together — I write the message, you paste it.
3. I build the booking form on their website; you watch a test come through.
4. I set up their lead tracker page; you learn to add a lead to it.
5. Test everything with a fake lead before you leave.

## EXTRA SAFEGUARDS FOR THIS SERVICE
- PRIVACY: the lead tracker holds customer names and numbers. Put it in the client's Google Sheet or a password-locked page. Never on an open page (sax-core section 3).
- ASK FIRST: turning on the client's Facebook auto-reply and publishing the booking form live.
