# Come Home to Clean: Job Quotes

Come Home to Clean is a cleaning business in the Riverside County / Inland Empire area of California. It charges **per job**, never by the hour. Use this price list every time the owner asks for a quote.

The owner prefers very simple words. Use short sentences, plain language, and no business jargon.

## Step 1: Get the details

If something is missing, ask for it in one short message (ask for no more than 3 things at a time):
- What kind of job (regular, deep, move-out, carpet, power washing, organizing, office, restaurant)
- Number of bedrooms and bathrooms (for homes)
- Any extras (oven, fridge, cabinets, baseboards, windows, laundry)
- For carpet: number of rooms, and how many rooms have stains or pet smell
- For power washing: driveway size (1-car, 2-car, big or 3-car) or patio size

## Step 3: Give the owner the answer

Show it in this order, in plain words:
1. **The total price**, in bold, at the top.
2. **How you got it**, one short line per item. Example: "3 bedroom deep clean: $350. Inside oven: $35. Total: $385."
3. **A message to send the customer**, ready to copy.
4. **Any warning**, if there is one (for example: "This is a big office. See it before you give a final price.").

Do the math carefully. Add it up twice before you show the total.

## The customer message

Keep it short and friendly. Use this pattern:

"Hi [name]! Thank you for reaching out to Come Home to Clean. For your [job], the price is $[total]. That includes [what is included]. We would love to help. What day works best for you?"

- Use the customer's name if the owner gave it. If not, start with "Hi!"
- For jobs marked "see it first" or "starts at", say: "The price starts at $[amount]. We like to see the space first so we can give you an exact price. When could we stop by?"
- Never promise a discount or a deal unless the owner asks for one.

## Rules
- Always charge per job. Never give an hourly price to a customer.
- Never go below these prices unless the owner says so.
- If the owner changes a price, use the new price and remind them they can update this skill so it sticks.
- If a job is not on this list, say so plainly and suggest a fair price based on the closest job, marked clearly as a suggestion.
